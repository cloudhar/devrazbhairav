Devraz Bhairav

Bhairava in the Bhairava Tantra of Kashmiri Shaivism represents three aspects of Shiva: Srishti (manifestation), Sthiti (maintenance), and Samhara (withdrawal).Ashta Bhairavas (Eight Bhairavas) are manifestations of the Bhairava. They guard and control the eight directions. The eight Bhairavas represent eight different elements in which five elements represented by Bhairava are sky, air, fire, water, earth, sun, moon, and Atma (the soul). Each Bhairava has eight sub Bhairavas under him (total 64).

The concept of 'Bhairavas' in Kashmir came during the rule of King Praversen II in sixth century, who desired to build a new city of his own and gave name to it. However the city was prone to floods, fires and epidemics. He worshipped his Ishta Dev, Vetal Bhairav. With his blessings, the kind laid the doundation of the new city and divided the city of Srinagar into eight zones, which were precised over by the respective 8 Bhairavas. temples were constructed in their names.

These Bhairavas were regarded as the protectors of their respective areas. A few of the 8 Bhairav in Srinagar are Anadeshwar Bhairav (Maisuma), Bhukatkeshwar Bhairav (Chattabal), Nandkeshwar Bhairav (Sumbal), and Vetalraj Bhairav (Dal Lake) to name a few. Further 64 Bhairav temples were constructed around Kashmir, which were protectors of their respective villages.

Devraz Bhairav, our Ishta Bhairav, is one of the 64 Bhairav and is guardian of our Nunar village for our heritage and traditions. His presence has been a guiding force, helping us navigate through adversities with resilience. In our Kashmiri Pandit tradition after every Puja ritual, we offer Naivedya/ Prasadam to the Ishta Devata and the Ishta Bhairava, the guardian deity of the area.

Devraz Bhairav Timeline

  1. 1940

    Devraz Bairav existed in the form of an idol and a Shiv Ling under a Kashmiri Bargad (Brann) tree in the open. Meanwhile the youths of Nunar formed a committee through a proper voting process, and Late Sh. Laxmanji Pandita, son of the Late Shri Raja Pandit, was elected as president.

  2. 1945

    After completing 5 year term, elections were held again and Late Sh. Prathvi Nath Pandita was elected as president. During his tenure, the present standing Dharamshalla was constructed at Vedsewar Temple. A Rath Yatra of Lord Krishna was started from Vedsewar Mandir to Devraz Bairav Temple on Janamashtami, annually.

  3. 1962

    Under the presidentship of Late Jawahar Lal Koul, a boundary wall was constructed around Devraz Bhairav Temple.

  4. 1963

    Asthapna Sudhar Samiti Nunar came into existence under the presidentship of Late Shri Balji Sharma and patron Late Shri Dina Nath Zutshi.

  5. 1980–1985

    The present standing Devraz Bhairav Temple was constructed under the presidentship of Late Makhan Lal Dhar.

  6. 1985–1990

    In 1985, elections were held and Shri Ram Krishan Raina was elected as president of Asthapan Sudhar Samiti, with Late Shri Sham Lal Raina as patron. With the mass exodus of Kashmiri Pandit community in 1990, only a handful of Pandit families stayed back to care for the temple.

  7. 2014

    Some construction purely for commercial purposes was carried out by the families who had not migrated from Nunar, for the maintenance of Devraz Bhairav Temple.

  8. 2024

    Nunar Biradhari members who have relocated across India and around the world started the mission to revive and restore the legacy of Devraz Bhairav by rebuilding the temple again.
