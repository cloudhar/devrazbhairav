About Devraz Bhairav Temple

Welcome to our website, the central hub for reconnecting with the roots of Nunar village. The name "Nunar" comes from “Nar” and “Narayan,” symbolizing the human and divine elements.

Our mission is to revive and restore the legacy of Devraz Bhairav, the revered deity of Nunar. Following the migration of Kashmiri Pandits, the temple fell into disrepair.

Devraz Bhairav is not just a deity but a guardian of our traditions, symbolizing strength, protection, and resilience. Join us in restoring this sacred heritage.
